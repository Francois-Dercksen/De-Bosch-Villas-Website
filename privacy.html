Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 September 2026

1. Introduction

De Bosch Villas ("us", "we", or "our") operates this website and takes your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your personal information when you visit our website, make an enquiry, or book a stay with us, in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

By using our website or providing us with your information, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address, and phone number, when you make an enquiry or booking.
  • Booking details, such as your stay dates, number of guests, and any special requests.
  • Payment information necessary to process a deposit or booking payment, which is handled by our payment processors and is not stored on our servers.
  • Usage data collected automatically when you visit our website, such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect to:

  • Process and confirm your bookings and respond to your enquiries.
  • Communicate with you about your stay, including check-in details and property policies.
  • Improve our website and the services we offer.
  • Comply with our legal and regulatory obligations.
  • Send you marketing communications, but only where you have opted in to receive them.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the basis that it is necessary to fulfil a booking or respond to your enquiry, to comply with our legal obligations, or because you have given your consent, such as when opting in to marketing communications.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate correctly and to understand how visitors use the site. You can set your browser to refuse cookies, though some parts of the website may not function properly if you do so.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with:

  • Trusted service providers who assist us with website hosting, booking administration, or payment processing, and who are bound to keep your information confidential.
  • Public authorities, where required to do so by South African law.
  • A successor entity, in the event of a sale or transfer of the De Bosch Villas business.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including any period required to meet our legal, accounting, or reporting obligations.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, unauthorised access, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

In accordance with POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal retention obligations.
  • Object to the processing of your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe your personal information has been mishandled.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below. We may ask you to verify your identity before responding to your request.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and Service are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can remove it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page along with a revised effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your data protection rights, please contact us at villas@debosch.co.za or call +27 73 528 2352.
