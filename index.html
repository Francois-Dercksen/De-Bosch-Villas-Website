About

Thoughtful design, natural beauty

Positioned on the front step of fenced pastures, overlooking Stellenbosch's most prestigious vineyards, De Bosch Villas offer an exclusive escape for leisure and rest. With a family-owned, active Saddlebred Equestrian facility as most beautiful backdrop for your retreat, effortless comfort, thoughtful design and quality nature surroundings await your indulgence.

Setting a new standard for luxury villas in the Winelands - we welcome you to our piece of paradise.
